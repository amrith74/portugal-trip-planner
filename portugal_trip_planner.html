🎓 Learn as you plan: Social & Technological Tourism

Tourism is more than choosing attractions. It affects communities and increasingly depends on digital tools.

👥 Social tourism

Looks at how tourism affects people and communities: jobs, local businesses, culture, accessibility, crowding and housing.

Think about: How can visitors support local people while respecting their daily lives?

📱 Tourism technology

Digital maps, booking websites, online reviews, contactless tickets, transport apps and virtual guides help people plan and experience trips.

Think about: How can technology make a trip easier, safer or more accessible?

♿ Accessibility

Accessible tourism considers whether transport, accommodation and attractions work for people with different mobility, sensory and communication needs.

Think about: Check verified access information, not just star ratings.

Real-world challenge: be a responsible trip planner

Imagine a popular town is crowded during summer. Choose public transport where practical, visit at quieter times, respect residential areas, and spend at locally owned businesses. Use official transport updates and recent reviews to make informed choices.

Key idea: Unlimited money does not mean unlimited resources. Good tourism balances visitor enjoyment with community wellbeing and environmental care.
